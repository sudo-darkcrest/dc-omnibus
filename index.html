Datacenter Omnibus

Welcome! This is a complete, hands-on course in how technology works, from the parts inside a computer to networks, fiber, data centers, security, service management and audit. Every module explains things in plain words first, then adds the exact terms the exams use. You'll find diagrams, interactive labs, quizzes and flashcards on every page.

It covers CompTIA A+ (220-1201/1202), Network+ (N10-009), Security+ (SY0-701), CySA+ (CS0-003), ITIL 4 Foundation, EPI DCCA, ISACA CISM, ISACA CISA, AWS Cloud Practitioner and AWS AI Practitioner, plus how AI works and where cyber security careers lead. New to tech? Start at Module 1 and go in order. Studying for one exam? Pick it below to see just its modules, or open the Study Hub for a plan.
