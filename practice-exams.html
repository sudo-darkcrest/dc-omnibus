Practice exams

Pick a certification and a length, switch on the timer if you want real-exam pace, and press Start exam. Questions come from every module and question bank, and are weighted like the real exam's domains where the vendor publishes weights. After you submit, you get a score for each domain with links to the module that teaches it.

Aim for 85% or more on several different practice runs before you book. Real exams use scaled scores and different questions, so a buffer helps. Exam-day advice is in the Study Hub tips.
